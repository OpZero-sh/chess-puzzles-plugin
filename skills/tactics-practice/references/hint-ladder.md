# Hint ladder

Give one level per request. Start at the lowest level the learner has not yet had on the current move. Go straight to a higher level only when they ask for it by name ("just tell me the piece", "show me the move"). When the line moves on to the learner's next move, the ladder starts again at level 1 for that move.

| Level | What to give | Example wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Look | A question that directs attention, with no chess content from the solution | "List every check and every capture you have. Which ones force a reply?" |
| 2. Change | What the opponent's last move changed: a square left unguarded, a line opened, a piece that moved away | "The last move took the queen off the back rank. What does that leave behind?" |
| 3. Target | The weak point, without the move | "The king on h8 has no squares. You only need a check it cannot answer." |
| 4. Idea | The name of the motif | "Look for a deflection of the rook." |
| 5. Piece | Which piece moves | "Your rook is the piece to move." |
| 6. Move | The first move, then let them play on | "It is Rxf3. What do you play after the queen takes back?" |
| 7. Line | The full solution with the reason for each move | Only on a clear request to see the answer or to give up |

## Rules

- Build each hint from the position and the stored line. Check a claim such as "undefended" or "no squares" on the board before saying it.
- Levels 1 and 2 must not narrow the answer to a single move.
- A puzzle requested as mate in one, two or three already tells the learner the goal. Level 3 for those is about where the king is trapped. Level 4 can name the mating pattern if a label gives one.
- After a wrong move, do not turn the refusal into a hint. Say it is not the move. If the learner asks why and the reason is short and concrete (the piece is simply taken, or the king escapes to a square you can name), show it. Otherwise say the puzzle line is different and offer a hint.
- Keep count of hints used on the puzzle and mention it in the tally if a session is running.
- Do not praise or scold. State what happened and what comes next.

## After the solve

1. Give the line in standard notation.
2. Name the idea and tie it to a move: "Qxh7+ is the attraction. It pulls the king onto the h file for the rook."
3. Say what the opponent's last move got wrong, when the position makes that clear.
4. Give one transferable cue: what to look for next time this pattern is on the board.
5. Repeat the puzzle ID.
